About

Quality should be built into the release process, not checked at the end.

I specialize in manual testing for mobile and web applications, with long-term experience supporting products through regression cycles, end-to-end validation, exploratory testing, functional testing, and release preparation. My focus is to make sure the product works as expected before it reaches users, especially in the areas that matter most to the business.

I work best with SaaS companies, growth-stage startups, and teams that need reliable ongoing QA support without building a large in-house QA function first. I bring a practical testing mindset, clear communication, and documentation that helps engineering teams understand risk, fix faster, and release with more confidence.

As teams adopt AI-assisted coding tools, QA becomes the final validation layer. Recent projects have expanded my work into pull request validation, reproducing reported issues, verifying fixes, and structured feedback loops between developers, coding agents, and product teams before changes are merged or released.

Working from UTC+07

Services

How I help product teams reduce release risk

suite/release

Release Preparation

Full regression testing before launch, validation of critical user journeys, and clear release reporting so stakeholders know what is ready and what still needs attention.

suite/ongoing

Ongoing QA Support

Weekly or sprint-based testing cycles, structured test case maintenance, exploratory testing, and disciplined retesting after bug fixes.

suite/product

Product-Focused Testing

Functional, usability, and edge-case testing that helps uncover logical gaps early and turns findings into bug reports the team can act on quickly.

suite/pull-requests

Pull Request Validation

Testing changes inside GitHub-based workflows before they merge: reproducing reported issues, verifying fixes, reviewing implementation outcomes, and leaving structured feedback on the PR.

suite/ai-assisted

AI-Assisted Development Validation

A human validation layer for teams shipping with coding agents: QA session orchestration, agent-assisted workflow checks, and clear feedback loops between developers, agents, and product.

How I Work
